How to read this table

MMLU Pro (60.0%) tests professional knowledge across dozens of subjects — respectable for the size, miles from frontier. GPQA (43.4%) is graduate science; Luna handles textbook recall and fails multi-step lab reasoning. LiveCodeBench (44.0%) is contest coding; simple scripts pass, repository surgery does not. Terminal-Bench Hard (3.0%) and GDPval (0.0%) measure real terminal and workplace agency — effectively zero, which is why this build removed computer control instead of pretending. Our 50/50 and 30/30 measure the harness around the weights: tools, refusals, formats — the part we actually built, and the part that makes daily use feel far stronger than 3%.
