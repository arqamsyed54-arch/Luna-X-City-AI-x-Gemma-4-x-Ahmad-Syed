02 — FROM QUESTION TO ANSWER

How a question travels.

1 · HEAR

Your words

Typed + Enter. Nothing runs silently.

2 · ROUTE

Right tool

Math, code, science, writing, web — or plain chat.

3 · CHECK

Verify first

Web evidence searched before any factual claim.

4 · THINK

Weigh & answer

Instruction-tuned reasoning, right-sized length.

5 · GUARD

Safety last look

Leaks stripped, refusals hold, answer printed.
