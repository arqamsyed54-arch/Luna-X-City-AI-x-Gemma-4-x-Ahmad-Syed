01 — The weights

google/gemma-4-E2B-it,
unmodified.

Dense, 35 layers, hidden 1536, GQA 8Q/1KV, 262K vocabulary, 128K context. Text, image and audio in. Apache-2.0, Google DeepMind.

WHERE THE PARAMETERS ARE

Measured off the weight headers: 5,123M.

More than half the model is vocabulary tables, not thinking. Token embeddings occupy 2.75 billion parameters while the thinking core — attention plus feed-forward layers — is barely two billion. Per-layer embeddings give each of the 35 layers its own small lookup table, which is why the total (5.1B) far exceeds the effective count (2.3B).

Attention and memory

Eight query heads share a single key-value head (grouped-query attention), keeping the long-context memory footprint small. Position comes from rotary embeddings with a million-scale base angle on global layers, plus shared key-values across twenty layers — the engineering that stretches context to 131,072 tokens. On a laptop CPU that window is real but slow: a full long-context cache is gigabytes by itself.

Senses

Text is native. Images enter through a 16-layer vision tower (768-wide, 16-pixel patches) at variable resolution. Audio enters through a 12-layer encoder at 16kHz sampling; video is read as frame sequences. Luna runs the instruction-tuned variant (-it): it follows chat format, takes system roles, and reasons step by step when asked.

Training and languages

Pre-training mixed web documents, code, mathematics and images across 140+ languages with a January 2025 cutoff — exactly why Luna verifies fresh facts on the web instead of trusting memory. Apache-2.0, from Google DeepMind's Gemma 4 family (E2B, E4B, 12B, 26B-A4B, 31B).
