05 — No marketing

Honest report.

DONEShipped

Terminal + browser chat · exact math · web-grounded facts · safety locks + suites · memory · model card + license.

WEAKFailed

Novel puzzles and proofs · strict counting · long-doc reasoning · medical images (refused) · ~60s/answer CPU.

Positive effects

Free offline AI on consumer hardware; students get exact math and grounded facts; safety-first defaults; a reproducible open harness anyone can inspect. For classrooms and tinkerers without GPU budgets, that combination previously did not exist at this quality level.

Negative effects & risks

Wrong answers on novel topics — verify important claims. Slow inference tempts unsafe shortcuts. Small-model overconfidence without web lookup. Open weights can be re-harnessed without these guards. The guards ship as code, not as guarantees — and the scariest failure is a confident wrong answer believed too quickly, which is why every factual answer carries its verification habit visibly.
